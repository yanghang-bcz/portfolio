Font name
  Huiwen-mincho / 汇文明朝体
  Version 1.00 (January 14, 2021)

Original author
  Terry Wang (特里王)
  Glyphs derived from scanned documents of the 1950s–60s Chinese character
  reform publications, including the "汉字简化方案草案" printed in 文汇报.
  Style: period letterpress / 旧铅字印刷.

Modifications made for this project
  Both changes below are permitted by CC0 1.0.

  1. Subset to the 3,500 most common simplified Chinese characters plus
     Latin, digits and punctuation, converted to WOFF2.
     Source: 24.8 MB TTF -> 2.1 MB WOFF2.

  2. The three punctuation glyphs below were translated inside their em box so
     that they sit at the lower-left corner, the Chinese convention, instead of
     the centre, which is the Japanese convention used by the upstream font.
     No other outline data was altered.

       U+3002 。  moved (-262, -186)   box 361,215,702,533 -> 99,29,440,347
       U+FF0C ，  moved (-286, -150)
       U+3001 、  moved (-278, -178)

     The upstream font has no `locl` feature, so the browser cannot switch
     punctuation position by language tag; the outlines themselves had to move.
